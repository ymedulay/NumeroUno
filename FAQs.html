Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for multiple jobs at the same time? +
You may apply for multiple jobs. You can view the statuses of your job applications at 'Application Status' which is found in the sidebar of your dashboard.
How do I delete my account? +
To delete your account, locate the User dropdown menu in the navigation bar, then go to 'My Account' and click 'Account Management' in the sidebar. This will direct you to your account recovery and account deletion. Look for 'Account Deletion' and follow the steps.
How does the website ensure the credibility of job postings? +
Credibilities of job postings and Business approvals are thoroughly checked and verified before it is publicly posted to the website.
What are the requirements to create an account? +
For applicants, creating an account only requires an email address and user's name. For businesses, creating an account requires a business email address, business name, and business email address.
Who do I contact for support? +
Reach out to us via the Contact Us page or check out the FAQs page to see if your inquiry has been answered.
